Billing Questions

New accounts can use a 7-day free trial. After the trial, continued access uses a Pro or Business plan. Prices are shown on the homepage pricing section. Choosing a paid plan does not start a charge yet, because online payment setup is still being prepared. Billing will depend on a supported payment provider when that setup is available.

Account Questions

Password changes and sign out are in Settings → Security. Export My Data is in Settings → Data & Privacy. Delete Account is in Settings → Danger Zone and asks you to type DELETE before it continues. Those actions stay available from Settings after a trial ends.

Review Link / QR Help

Create a business from Review Links. The trial includes 1 business, Pro includes up to 5, and Business includes up to 25. QR & NFC uses a review link that already exists. If none is available yet, create one from Review Links first.

FAQ

How does the 7-day trial work?

A new account can receive one free trial that lasts 7 days and includes 1 business. When the trial ends, Dashboard, Analytics, Feedback, Review Links, and QR & NFC require an active Pro or Business plan. Settings stays open.

How do I create a review link?

Sign in, open Review Links, and create a business. ReviewRise checks your plan limit before saving a new business. You can then use that link for your review page, QR code, or NFC tag.

Why did my trial expire?

The free trial lasts 7 days from the trial end time saved on the account. After that, pages that need an active plan show the trial lock unless Pro or Business is active. Your feedback and businesses are not deleted. Open Settings → Subscription to see the plan on the account.

How do I upgrade?

Open Settings → Subscription and review Pro or Business. The buttons explain that online payment setup is coming soon. They do not activate a paid plan or start billing.

How does Customer Intelligence work?

Customer Intelligence reads the written feedback already stored for your businesses and groups similar comments into recurring themes. It is a way to review patterns. It does not change ratings or promise more reviews, revenue, or a higher Google rating.

Can I use ReviewRise for multiple businesses?

Yes, within the plan limit. The trial supports 1 business, Pro supports up to 5, and Business supports up to 25. The business switcher lets you focus on one business or view all of them.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Danger Zone and choose Delete Account. Type DELETE to enable the final button. The account is removed only after ReviewRise confirms the deletion. If that step fails, you stay signed in.

How do I export my data?

Open Settings → Data & Privacy and choose Export My Data. When the export service is available, ReviewRise downloads a JSON file of your own profile, businesses, feedback, and subscription details. The file does not include your password or sign-in tokens.
