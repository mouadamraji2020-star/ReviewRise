If you manage your business on Google

This is the quickest way. Google gives you the link itself.

  1. Sign in to the Google account that manages your business.
  2. Search your business name on Google or Google Maps.
  3. Click Ask for reviews, then copy the link.

Create it from your Google Maps link

Works for any business on Google Maps, even if you don't manage it.

  1. Open your business on Google Maps and click Share.
  2. Click Copy link, paste it below and click Create review link.
