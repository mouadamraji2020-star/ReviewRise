How does ReviewRise work?

Customers scan your QR code or tap your NFC tag and rate their stay from 1 to 5 stars. Happy customers are sent to your Google review page. Unhappy ones leave private feedback that only you can read.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new account gets a 7-day free trial with 1 business. No payment is needed to start.

How much does it cost?

Pro is $20 per month or $192 per year and includes up to 5 businesses. Business is $49 per month or $470 per year and includes up to 25 businesses. Prices are in US dollars.

How do I pay for a paid plan?

Paid plans are arranged on WhatsApp. Message us, we send you the payment details, and your plan is activated once your payment is confirmed.

Do I need special equipment?

No. A printed QR code works with any phone camera. NFC tags are optional if you want customers to tap instead of scan.

Can I choose which ratings go to Google?

Yes. In your review page settings you choose the rating from which customers are sent to Google, and you can turn private feedback on or off.

Who can see the private feedback?

Only you, from the Feedback page in your account. It is not shown publicly.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. In Settings you can download your account data and delete your account.
