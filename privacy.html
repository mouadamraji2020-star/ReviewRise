Privacy Policy

This policy describes how ReviewRise handles information when you use the ReviewRise website and application.

Introduction

ReviewRise helps businesses collect private customer feedback and understand it inside their account. This policy applies to account holders, the businesses they add, and customers who submit feedback through a ReviewRise review page.

Information We Collect

ReviewRise collects the information needed to create an account, operate the product, and show a business its own feedback. The sections below describe the categories of that information.

Account Information

When you create or manage a ReviewRise account, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, email address, and account preferences. We also keep a record of whether onboarding is complete and which subscription state applies to the account.

Business Information

If you add a business, ReviewRise stores the business details you enter, such as the business name, logo, and the review link you choose to share. That information is used to present the correct business on review pages, review links, and QR or NFC materials.

Customer Feedback

Customers may submit a rating and written feedback through a public ReviewRise review page. ReviewRise stores that feedback so the business account can view, filter, and analyze it. Feedback is associated with the review link the customer used.

Support Messages

When you send a message through the Help Center, or report a problem from Settings, ReviewRise stores your name, your email address, the subject and text of your message, the category you chose, and the page you wrote from. If you are signed in, the message is linked to your account. A one-way code derived from your network (IP) address is also saved, only to limit spam. These messages are used to answer you and to improve the service. They are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and keep support records, and you can ask for yours to be deleted by contacting ReviewRise.

Preventing Repeated and Fake Ratings

To stop the same person from rating a business repeatedly and to stop automated abuse, ReviewRise saves two technical details when a customer submits a rating. The first is a random identifier created in the customer’s browser; it contains no name or contact detail. The second is a code derived from the customer’s network (IP) address using a one-way function, so the address itself is not stored and cannot be read back from the code. These details are used only to apply limits on repeated ratings and floods, are not shown to the business, are cleared out periodically (normally after about 60 days), and are removed when the business is deleted. Because a hotel or café often shares one network among many guests, the limits are set so that normal use by several guests is not blocked.

Analytics Data

ReviewRise calculates summaries from the feedback already stored for an account, such as rating totals, trends, and recurring themes. Those summaries are produced so the business can read its own results. They are not sold as a separate data product.

Authentication

Sign-in is handled through ReviewRise’s authentication provider. A session is kept in the browser so you can stay signed in. Authentication tokens are used only to confirm that a request belongs to your account.

Payment Information

ReviewRise does not process card payments inside the website and does not store card numbers. Paid plans are currently arranged by message: when you choose a paid plan, ReviewRise opens a prepared WhatsApp message containing the plan you chose, your billing period, and the email address of your ReviewRise account, so that the plan can be matched to your account. You then pay as instructed (for example by bank transfer), and ReviewRise activates your plan after the payment is confirmed. Messages sent on WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. ReviewRise stores the plan name, billing period, subscription status, and the end date of the paid period so that your account shows the correct access. If an online payment provider is connected in the future, this section will be updated before it is used.

Cookies / Local Storage

ReviewRise uses browser local storage to keep the sign-in session, the selected business, and short-lived copies of information already loaded for that account. This lets the app reopen without asking you to sign in on every page. You can clear this storage in your browser. Clearing it signs you out of ReviewRise on that device. Customers who open a review page do not sign in. On their device ReviewRise stores the random identifier described above and a note that a business was already rated, for up to 30 days, so that the page can tell them they have already shared their feedback.

How We Use Information

ReviewRise uses information to:

  • create and secure your account
  • show your businesses, feedback, analytics, review links, and QR or NFC tools
  • apply the trial or paid plan associated with the account
  • remember the business you selected on this device
  • answer the messages you send to support
  • maintain and protect the service

Access by ReviewRise

The person who operates ReviewRise can see account details through a restricted administration page, protected by a separate sign-in check and passcode. This includes the email address and name on an account, when it was created, its last sign-in, its plan, and counts such as the number of businesses and feedback entries. It also lists the support messages that people send to ReviewRise, so they can be answered. It is used to run the service, give support, and activate paid plans. That page does not display the written content of customer feedback.

Data Storage

Account, business, and feedback records are stored in a hosted database. Files such as business logos are stored in hosted file storage connected to the same infrastructure. Access to those records is limited to the signed-in account that owns them, subject to the security controls of the service.

Third-Party Services

ReviewRise uses Supabase as its infrastructure, authentication, and database provider. Supabase processes account and application data so ReviewRise can sign users in and store the records described in this policy. When payments are enabled, a separate payment provider may process payment information. ReviewRise does not control the provider’s own systems.

Data Retention

ReviewRise keeps account, business, and feedback information for as long as the account remains active and the information is needed to operate the service. Cached copies in the browser remain until they are replaced, you sign out, or you clear site data.

Security

ReviewRise uses authenticated access, hosted infrastructure controls, and browser session storage to protect account data. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and ReviewRise does not promise that unauthorized access can never occur.

User Rights

You may review and update the business and account details available in ReviewRise settings. In Settings you can also change your password, download a copy of your data (your profile, businesses, feedback, and subscription details) as a file, and delete your account. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, your businesses and their logos, the feedback stored for them, your payment records, and your sign-in. Copies kept in the hosting provider’s backups may remain for a limited period before they are overwritten. You may also clear ReviewRise data stored in your browser. Other requests about your information can be made through the contact method below.

International Users

ReviewRise may be used from different countries. Information may be processed in the countries where ReviewRise’s infrastructure providers operate. Those locations can differ from the country where you or your customers are located.

Changes to Policy

ReviewRise may update this policy as the product changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. Continued use of ReviewRise after an update means the updated policy applies.
